Day 4 Mon, 16 Nov 2026 Alishan Base (Night 1)
Xiding Tea Trail ➔ Fenqihu Bento ➔ Alishan High Forest
  • #7 Eryanping Trail (Xiding)
  • #6 Fenqihu Old Street
  • #12 Alishan National Forest Recreation Area
  • 🏨 Alishan Shermuh Hotel / Gau Shan Ching (Night 1 of 2)
  • 🍲 Shanbin Restaurant (Alishan)
  • Depart Chiayi early via mountain Bus 7322 ascending the misty Alishan Highway.
  • Stop at Xiding to hike the Eryanping Trail (Pin #7) timber boardwalk through high-mountain tea plantations and sea-of-clouds viewing pavilions.
  • Continue to Fenqihu Old Street (Pin #6); stroll through historic cedar railway tracks and savor the famous authentic Fenqihu Railway Bento in a classic tin box.
  • Afternoon mountain bus up to Alishan National Forest Recreation Area (Pin #12) gate at 2,200m elevation.
  • Check in at Alishan Shermuh Hotel / Gau Shan Ching (2-night base: 16–18 Nov).
  • Relaxed late afternoon stroll around Alishan Station and Zhaoping Park as alpine dusk settles in.
  • Dinner: Hearty stone hot pot at Shanbin Restaurant in Alishan Village.

Day 5 Tue, 17 Nov 2026 Alishan Base (Night 2)
Zhushan Sunrise ➔ Sacred Red Cypresses ➔ Alishan Forest
  • #10 Zhushan Sunrise Trail & Station
  • #11 Giant Tree Cluster Trail
  • #12 Alishan National Forest Recreation Area
  • 🌲 Shuishan Giant Tree Trail
  • 🏨 Alishan Shermuh Hotel / Gau Shan Ching (Night 2 of 2)
  • 05:00 board the narrow-gauge Zhushan Sunrise Forest Train to Zhushan Station (Taiwan's highest station at 2,451m).
  • Walk 10 minutes past the platform to Ogasawara Mountain 360 Observation Deck for the breathtaking golden sunrise lighting up the peaks of Yushan (Jade Mountain) above the sea of clouds.
  • Walk down the tranquil Zhushan Sunrise Trail (or return train) to Alishan Village for a warm breakfast.
  • Full unhurried day trekking Alishan's legendary alpine trail network without any luggage: Giant Tree Cluster Trail (Boardwalks #1 and #2) visiting 36 colossal red cypresses over 1,000–2,000 years old.
  • Visit the tranquil emerald waters of Sister Ponds, the ancient hollow Three Generation Tree, and historic Shouzhen Temple.
  • Trek the peaceful moss-carpeted Shuishan Giant Tree Trail along historic decommissioned logging rails to the 2,700-year-old Shuishan sacred cedar.
  • Ride the nostalgic Shenmu (Sacred Tree) Line or Zhaoping Line forest train.
  • Relaxed afternoon sampling fragrant high-mountain Alishan oolong tea in Alishan Village.
  • Dinner in Alishan Village; peaceful second night at Alishan Shermuh Hotel / Gau Shan Ching.

Day 6 Wed, 18 Nov 2026 Sun Moon Lake & Taichung
Alishan ➔ Sun Moon Lake (Boat Cruise & Bikeway) ➔ Taichung
  • #5 Sun Moon Lake (Xiangshan & Shuishe Pier)
  • 🌊 Ita Thao Lakeside Pier
  • 🚴 Sun Moon Lake Bikeway (CNN Top 10)
  • 🏨 Taichung Hotel Base (Night 1 of 2)
  • Optional second sunrise chance at Zhushan or leisurely morning waking up in the crisp mountain air of Alishan; breakfast and hotel check-out.
  • 09:00 board the scenic trans-mountain transit from Alishan directly across the Central Mountain Range to Sun Moon Lake via Yuanlin Bus 6739 (scenic highway passing the Tataka / Yushan National Park border vista).
  • Arrive at Sun Moon Lake (Shuishe Pier) (Pin #5) around midday.
  • Enjoy a lakeside lunch featuring Thao indigenous specialty dishes in Shuishe Village.
  • Hop on the Sun Moon Lake Solar Ferry / Boat Cruise across the turquoise waters to Ita Thao Pier; stroll Ita Thao Shopping District for black tea treats and wild boar snacks.
  • Rent bicycles to pedal along the world-renowned Sun Moon Lake Bikeway stretching towards the architectural Xiangshan Visitor Center and panoramic Xiangshan Viewing Platform.
  • Late afternoon express coach (Taiwan Tourist Shuttle 6670) from Sun Moon Lake to Taichung City (~75 mins).
  • Check in at Taichung Hotel Base (2-night base: 18–20 Nov).
  • Dinner: Local culinary favorites in Taichung (e.g. Yizhong Street or central local dining).
